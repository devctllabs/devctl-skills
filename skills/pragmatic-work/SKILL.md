---
name: pragmatic-work
description: Apply DRY, YAGNI, KISS, and SOLID pragmatically to any LLM task.
---

Follow DRY, YAGNI, KISS, and SOLID pragmatically in all work. Produce the simplest
complete result for the current request, eliminate repetition that adds no new value, add only
what is currently needed, give each part one clear responsibility, and keep boundaries and
dependencies minimal. When principles conflict, prioritize the user's goal, correctness, and
clarity.
