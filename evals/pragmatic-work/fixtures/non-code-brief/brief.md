# Knowledge Base Migration Kickoff

Prepare a concise kickoff brief for a three-person team migrating an internal FAQ from a shared
document to a searchable knowledge base.

Include:

- the migration goal;
- one responsibility each for the product owner, content editor, and platform engineer;
- exactly three delivery steps;
- completion criteria.

The migration covers the existing FAQ only. Analytics, permissions redesign, and future
automation are out of scope.
